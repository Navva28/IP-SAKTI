FOOD SAFETY AND STANDARDS (AYURVEDA AAHARA) REGULATIONS, 2022

Source / Authority: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India
(FSSAI). Primary instrument: Food Safety and Standards (Ayurveda Aahara)
Regulations, 2022.
source_url = https://fssai.gov.in/
1.  DEFINITION “Ayurveda Aahara” means food prepared in accordance with
    recipes, ingredients or processes described in authoritative books
    of Ayurveda listed in Schedule A, including products with other
    botanical ingredients in accordance with the concept of Ayurveda
    Aahara.

2.  EXCLUSIONS The definition excludes specified categories including
    Ayurvedic drugs, proprietary Ayurvedic medicines, medicinal
    products, cosmetics, narcotic or psychotropic substances, herbs
    listed under Schedule E-1 of the Drugs and Cosmetics framework,
    metals-based Ayurvedic drugs or medicines, bhasma or pishti, and
    other ingredients notified by the Food Authority.

3.  AUTHORITATIVE BOOKS Schedule A identifies authoritative Ayurveda
    books relevant to the regulations.

4.  CATEGORIES Schedule B provides categories and requirements for
    Ayurveda Aahara. The Food Authority may provide lists of Ayurveda
    Aahara covered under relevant categories from time to time.

5.  OFFICIAL IMPLEMENTATION UPDATES FSSAI issued an order in July 2025
    identifying Ayurveda Aahara covered under Category A in consultation
    with the Ministry of Ayush. FSSAI also issued further recipe-related
    material in June 2026.

6.  MANUFACTURE Food Business Operators must formulate Ayurveda Aahara
    according to the applicable categories and requirements. Processes
    described in authoritative books may be adapted for industrial-scale
    manufacture and packing when the regulatory conditions are met.

7.  SAFETY Ayurveda Aahara must comply with applicable food-safety
    requirements concerning permitted ingredients, additives,
    contaminants, residues and other standards.

8.  CLAIMS The regulations contain requirements concerning claims and
    restrict treatment or cure claims that are not permitted under the
    food regulatory framework.

9.  LABELLING Ayurveda Aahara is subject to labelling and packaging
    requirements under the regulations and other applicable FSSAI
    requirements.

10. INTENDED USE Classification depends on the statutory definition and
    product facts, including ingredients, recipe or process, claims,
    intended purpose and applicable exclusions.

11. DIFFERENCE FROM ASU DRUGS Ayurveda Aahara is a food category
    regulated by FSSAI. ASU drugs are regulated under the Drugs and
    Cosmetics framework. Product classification depends on the
    applicable statutory definitions and facts.
