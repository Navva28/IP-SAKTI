WIPO TREATY ON INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, GENETIC RESOURCES AND ASSOCIATED
TRADITIONAL KNOWLEDGE

Source / Authority: World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO).
 source_url = https://www.wipo.int/
1.  TREATY The WIPO Treaty on Intellectual Property, Genetic Resources
    and Associated Traditional Knowledge was adopted on 24 May 2024.

2.  SUBJECT The treaty addresses intellectual property systems in
    relation to genetic resources and associated traditional knowledge.

3.  DISCLOSURE A central feature is a disclosure requirement concerning
    the country of origin or source of genetic resources when an
    invention is based on genetic resources.

Where an invention is based on associated traditional knowledge, the
treaty provides for disclosure concerning the source of that associated
traditional knowledge under the treaty framework.

4.  PURPOSE The disclosure system is intended to improve transparency
    concerning the relationship between patent applications and genetic
    resources or associated traditional knowledge.

5.  ENTRY INTO FORCE Adoption does not automatically make the treaty
    binding on every country. The treaty enters into force three months
    after the required number of ratifications or accessions specified
    in the treaty is reached.

6.  NATIONAL IMPLEMENTATION Practical effect depends on entry into force
    and national implementation. National patent and biodiversity laws
    remain relevant.

7.  INDIA The treaty should not automatically be treated as a
    universally binding Indian filing requirement without checking
    treaty status and Indian implementation. Current Indian patent and
    biodiversity law remains relevant.

8.  GENETIC RESOURCES Genetic resources can be relevant to inventions
    involving biological material, plants, microorganisms and other
    biological sources.

9.  ASSOCIATED TRADITIONAL KNOWLEDGE The treaty addresses disclosure of
    source or origin information in relevant cases. It does not itself
    create a worldwide patent.

10. PATENT SYSTEM The treaty does not replace national patent
    examination systems or grant a universal patent.

Source note: This is a factual summary of WIPO’s official description of
the 2024 treaty. Current treaty status and national implementation
should be checked when applying it to a specific jurisdiction.
