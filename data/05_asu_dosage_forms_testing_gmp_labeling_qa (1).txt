AYURVEDIC, SIDDHA AND UNANI DRUGS — DOSAGE FORMS, TESTING, GMP AND
LABELLING

Source / Authority: Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO),
Government of India. Primary framework: Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945
and official ASU testing and GMP material.
source_url = https://cdsco.gov.in/
1.  DOSAGE FORMS Official ASU testing and regulatory materials recognize
    dosage forms and categories including Asava and Arista, Avaleha,
    Guggulu preparations, Ghrita, Taila, Vati or Gutika, Bhasma, Lauha,
    Ghana Sattva, syrups, tablets, capsules, ointments, Kshar Sutra,
    single-drug preparations and other specified traditional dosage
    forms.

Exact requirements depend on the product and applicable legal category.

2.  TESTING Testing requirements vary according to dosage form,
    ingredients and applicable category. Official materials provide
    product-specific analytical parameters.

3.  QUALITY PARAMETERS Testing can address identity, purity, quality,
    physical characteristics, chemical parameters, microbial quality and
    other requirements specified for the product.

4.  GOOD MANUFACTURING PRACTICES Schedule T contains GMP requirements
    for ASU medicines, covering matters such as premises, sanitation,
    equipment, storage, raw materials, manufacturing controls, quality
    control, documentation and personnel.

5.  RAW MATERIALS Raw materials are subject to applicable quality
    requirements. Identity and quality of ingredients are important to
    finished-product quality.

6.  FINISHED PRODUCTS Finished products are subject to quality-control
    requirements appropriate to their category and dosage form.

7.  LABELLING ASU drug labelling is regulated under the Drugs and
    Cosmetics Rules. Requirements vary according to product category.

8.  ALCOHOL-CONTAINING PREPARATIONS The rules contain provisions
    concerning labelling and packing of relevant alcoholic ASU
    preparations such as Asava and Arista products.

9.  RECORDS Manufacturing and quality systems require records supporting
    identification and control of batches and materials as specified by
    applicable rules.

10. FOOD DISTINCTION Ayurveda Aahara is regulated separately as a food
    category under FSSAI. ASU drugs are regulated under the Drugs and
    Cosmetics framework.

11. PATENTABILITY Dosage form, GMP compliance or regulatory testing does
    not by itself create patent rights. Patentability depends on the
    claimed invention and the Patents Act.

Source note: This is a factual summary of official CDSCO ASU testing,
GMP and labelling material.
