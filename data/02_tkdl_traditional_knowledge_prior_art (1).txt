TRADITIONAL KNOWLEDGE DIGITAL LIBRARY (TKDL)

Source / Authority: Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL), CSIR
and Ministry of Ayush, Government of India.
 source_url= https://tkdl.res.in/
1.  PURPOSE TKDL documents Indian traditional knowledge associated with
    systems including Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, Sowa Rigpa and Yoga.

2.  DOCUMENTATION Traditional medicinal knowledge from authoritative
    traditional texts is transcribed and structured so that it can be
    used in patent-search and examination contexts.

3.  TRADITIONAL KNOWLEDGE RESOURCE CLASSIFICATION TKDL uses the
    Traditional Knowledge Resource Classification (TKRC) to organize
    traditional knowledge.

4.  SYSTEMS COVERED The documented systems include Ayurveda, Siddha,
    Unani, Sowa Rigpa and Yoga.

5.  DATABASE SIZE The official TKDL About material reports more than
    4.54 lakh formulations and practices. Database figures can change as
    documentation is updated.

6.  SOURCE LITERATURE TKDL documentation is based on classical and
    authoritative literature. Depending on the source, documented
    material can include ingredients, combinations, preparation methods,
    uses and other characteristics.

7.  PATENT SIGNIFICANCE Documented traditional knowledge can be relevant
    prior-art information. Section 3(p) of the Patents Act specifically
    excludes inventions that in effect are traditional knowledge or an
    aggregation or duplication of known properties of traditionally
    known components.

8.  ACCESS TKDL is not an unrestricted public search engine for all of
    its contents. Access and permitted use are governed by the framework
    established by the competent authorities.

9.  AYURVEDIC KNOWLEDGE Classical Ayurvedic literature can describe
    medicinal plants, minerals and other substances, single-drug uses,
    compound formulations, preparation methods, dosage forms,
    therapeutic uses and combinations.

10. DEFENSIVE DOCUMENTATION TKDL provides structured documentation that
    assists patent offices in identifying traditional knowledge during
    search and examination. Its role is documentation and defensive
    protection of traditional knowledge.

Source note: This document summarizes official TKDL information. Exact
current database counts and access conditions should be checked against
current official TKDL material.
