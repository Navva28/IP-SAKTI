PATENT COOPERATION TREATY (PCT) — INTERNATIONAL PATENT FILING ROUTE

Source / Authority: World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO).
 source_url = https://www.wipo.int/
1.  PURPOSE The PCT provides a procedure for filing a single
    international patent application that can serve as a filing route in
    multiple PCT contracting states.

2.  NO WORLDWIDE PATENT A PCT application does not create a worldwide
    patent. National or regional patent offices ultimately determine
    whether protection is granted under their laws.

3.  PRIORITY PERIOD The usual period for claiming priority from an
    earlier application is 12 months, subject to the applicable PCT and
    national requirements.

4.  INTERNATIONAL APPLICATION A PCT application contains information
    such as applicant details, title, description, claims, drawings
    where required, abstract and priority information where claimed.

5.  INTERNATIONAL SEARCH A PCT application is subject to an
    international search by an International Searching Authority. The
    search identifies relevant prior-art documents.

6.  INTERNATIONAL PUBLICATION PCT applications are generally published
    internationally according to the PCT publication system.

7.  INTERNATIONAL PRELIMINARY EXAMINATION The PCT system provides
    optional international preliminary examination procedures. These
    procedures provide information concerning patentability but do not
    themselves grant a patent.

8.  NATIONAL PHASE After the international phase, the applicant must
    enter the national or regional phase in the countries or regions
    where protection is sought.

9.  NATIONAL-PHASE TIMING A common PCT national-phase time limit is 30
    months from the priority date, although applicable requirements can
    vary by country and situation.

10. INDIA India is a PCT contracting state. Entry into the Indian
    national phase requires compliance with Indian patent law, rules and
    applicable PCT national-phase requirements.

11. BENEFIT The PCT provides a coordinated international filing and
    search procedure and can postpone multiple national filings.

12. AYURVEDA An Ayurveda-related invention seeking protection in
    multiple countries may use the PCT route when the relevant
    requirements are satisfied. Patentability and traditional-knowledge
    or biodiversity requirements remain jurisdiction-specific.

Source note: This document summarizes the official WIPO PCT system.
Current national-phase requirements should be checked for each target
jurisdiction.
